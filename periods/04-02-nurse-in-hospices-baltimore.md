## Stories and small details

- **Nursing studies**: Lillian studies nursing in the United States from 2006 to 2008, then works as a nurse in hospices and nursing homes, from about 2009 to 2013.
- **Seasons Hospice (Baltimore)**: her hospice job in Maryland.
- **The desk job at « Hospice Care » (about 2013, for 6 months)**: a for-profit agency. She sits in an office with a phone and a computer. A « liaison nurse » goes to hospitals, talks to doctors and gives the names of dying patients; the families fill in a file and fax it. Lillian checks medically that the patient qualifies (life expectancy under 6 months, with the doctor's validation) and checks the insurance. The pressure is on numbers: at least 25 admissions a day, a board on the wall, objectives and bonuses. She feels she is « selling beds » to the dying, « the agent of death ». Some cases are very young (17-18). She works with a stomach ache, hates working in an office and ends up deeply bothered.
- **The decision to change**: the death of her American grandmother in 2013 shakes her; at 33, she feels that the job « eats her ». She listens to podcasts, wants to travel, and makes a list of jobs that would let her travel; flight attendant is one of them (see [05-01](05-01-flight-attendant.md)).
