## People around her

### [Lillian](../people/lillian.md)

- **Role during this period**: Main character, 18; goes back to the United States to find her father, lives at his place in North Syracuse, and leaves after Mark's non-answer to Clara's ultimatum

### [Dad](../people/dad.md)

- **Role during this period**: lives in North Syracuse, aged 46. He welcomes Lillian at his home and is more than delighted.
- **Life anecdotes**: he is not at the airport when she arrives, because he went to the wrong one. He calls her « Soussoune » (his nickname for her) and bounces her on his knees at 18, as he did when she was little. He is also afraid she will take the bus at 18, as if she were 10.
- **Emotionally strong or deep stories in the relationship**: he tells her in the first days that it is hard to look at her because she looks too much like Béatrice, whom he is still in love with. He is full of remorse for having lost his family, but with no distance on the fact he could have taken his destiny in hand. He never explained to Lillian why he did not set up a home for his wife and children in the United States, or join them in France if that was better. When Clara gives him an ultimatum, he hesitates and gives no answer.

### [Clara](../people/clara.md)

- **Role during this period**: Mark's partner. Born in 1950, of Puerto Rican origin, a teacher. He meets her through a newspaper personal ad, which Lillian had encouraged him to place.
- **Life anecdotes**: after a phone call following the ad, they meet at a Denny's. At the beginning of the relationship, Mark is happy with her.
- **Emotionally strong or deep stories in the relationship**: she views the closeness between Mark and Lillian unfavourably. One day she asks Mark to choose between her and Lillian, which means Lillian leaving the house. The Clara story is a disappointment, a wound for Lillian.

## Stories and small details

- **The arrival (11 September 1998)**: Mark is not at the airport, because he went to the wrong one.
- **The welcome**: Mark lives in North Syracuse and welcomes her at his place, more than delighted.
- **Time stopped**: Lillian has the impression that time stopped when she was little. Mark, 46, speaks only about the past and about her mother. In the first days he tells her it is hard to look at her because she looks too much like Béatrice, whom he is still in love with. He has never been able to turn the page, even today.
- **Mark's routine**: Lillian is saddened to find him in a very simple routine, watching films and reading books almost all the time. She remembers seeing him look at the wall, just to pass the time.
- **« Soussoune »**: he calls her « Soussoune » (his nickname for her) and bounces her on his knees at 18, as he did when she was 6. He is also afraid she will take the bus at 18, as if she were 10. Lillian wants to make up for the lost time and plays along, being still a little girl in her head, but she never told herself that time could be made up.
- **Remorse without perspective**: he regrets having lost his family, but has no distance on the fact he could have taken his destiny in hand. He never explained his choices to Lillian: not setting up a home in the United States for his wife and children, nor joining them in France if that was better.
- **School**: Lillian has her French baccalauréat, but Mark recommends she go to high school in the United States. She lasts 2 weeks: the atmosphere and the shock compared to the French system and her boarding school in La Baule.
- **The supermarket job**: she then takes a job as a cashier at the "Price Shopper" at the end of the street (a 20-minute walk). She has a crush on a guy who works there, one of her first crushes. She resigns because of a misunderstanding (« quiproco ») and has always avoided that store since.
- **Clara and the plan**: it was Lillian's idea that Mark place a personal ad and meet people. When Clara enters his life, Mark is happy, and Lillian is happy that her plan worked and her father has found a partner.
- **The ultimatum**: Lillian stays about a year and a half after Clara arrives. Clara views the closeness between them unfavourably, and one day asks Mark to choose between her and Lillian (so Lillian would have to leave the house).
- **The non-answer**: Mark tells Lillian about the ultimatum. When she asks what he has decided, he hesitates and does not say. Lillian decides to leave Syracuse on that non-answer.
- Life in Syracuse comes before New York City.
