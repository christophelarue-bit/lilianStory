## Stories and small details

- Raised by her French grandparents, Papouche and Mamouche, in a reserved Saint-Brevin family: « ma chérie », four kisses, no hugs and no « je t'aime » said aloud, although she always felt the affection. A contrast with her American grandmother, the "mega hug".
- At table, Mamouche is the mistress of the house with her armchair: everyone waits for her to sit before sitting down.
- Every Christmas, Grandma Lillian (the paternal grandmother) sends the children a huge parcel of gifts from the United States, from Lillian's 6 years onward. Mark never did; Grandma earned almost nothing and bought the gifts in January, with the sales.
- Lillian keeps a slight American accent: first language English, then French.
- Around 1995, at 16, Lillian and Gwen go on holiday to New York to see their father.
- Lillian shows off her little brother Xavier to her friends, finding him super intelligent, though he is 6 years younger.
- Lillian attends a boarding school in La Baule (years to clarify) and passes her French baccalauréat.
