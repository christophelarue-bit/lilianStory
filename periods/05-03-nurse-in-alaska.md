## Stories and small details

- **Why Alaska**: she has always dreamed of living in Alaska, even during her marriage to [Mohamed](../people/mohamed.md), who was not interested at all. She was obsessed, « like a calling »: the opposite of Maui, hostile and wild, cold, with months of darkness then months of daylight, bears, Republican, not for everyone. Nature has always been her therapy. She had only been to Anchorage once as a flight attendant.
- **How**: she works through nursing agencies (temporary assignments), which find missions anywhere in the United States, even in Australia. Each state requires its own licence and sometimes extra classes: Hawaii took her about 4 months; Alaska was easy because she was so determined.
- **The trigger**: José leaves for Spain at the end of the Gillette stay ([José](../people/jose.md)). Devastated but unwilling to leave the United States, she decides to go to Alaska first, planning to join him in Spain 6 months later; the COVID closes the borders.
- **Contract**: she arrives in June 2020 for 3 months, then renews for 3 more.
- **The borders and the French passport**: she wants to return to Europe to be closer to José, who is in Spain, but the borders are closed. She has no French passport and no US passport (she is a US resident, registered in Hawaii). The French embassy tells her that she must do like everyone. She decides to apply for a French passport, which she might never have done otherwise. As a resident of Hawaii, her consulate is San Francisco.
- **The appointments**: every morning for one or two weeks she checks the consulate website, which always shows zero slots. At last she gets one, takes leave from work, and flies from Alaska to San Francisco for 2 days with a hotel.
- **At the consulate**: a very kind official types her name: she does not exist in the system. In tears, she begs him to speak to her mother. [Béatrice](../people/mom.md) picks up despite the time difference, and he talks with her. He does not even tell her it is guaranteed.
- **The passport**: sent to [Mike](../people/mike.md) in Hawaii, who forwards it to Alaska by FedEx. She chose him rather than David because he is reliable and rigorous.
- She no longer knows which state she preferred, Alaska or Hawaii.
- Lillian is a nurse in Seward, by the sea.
- The COVID happens during this period, but she discovers the nature of Alaska.
- She crosses paths with a bear in the wild.
