## Era summary

At 18, Lillian goes back to the United States to find her father, whom she has not seen day to day since 1985. She finds him full of remorse and with no wish to rebuild his life, lives at his place and encourages him to meet women. He meets Clara. Father and daughter find each other after 15 years apart, as if time had been on pause for both of them. Clara views their closeness unfavourably and asks Mark to choose between her and Lillian. Mark hesitates and gives no answer, and Lillian decides to leave Syracuse on that non-answer, less than 2 years after arriving.

She carries on her life in New York City, where she meets David, a brief Chinese flirt who lives in the city, and Walter, from Bangladesh. It is also in New York that she meets Mohamed, her future husband.
