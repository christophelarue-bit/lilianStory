## Era summary

Lillian becomes a flight attendant, then a nurse in Hawaii, then in Alaska. Three very different periods. In Alaska, she is a nurse in Seward, by the sea: the COVID, but also the nature (a bear met in the wild, road trips to Fairbanks, a flight over the mountains, fishing boats at sunset) and friends in the same building ([Kim](../people/kim.md) and a gay friend) with their pyjama parties.

It is during this era that she meets Adriana, [Kim](../people/kim.md) and [David (Hawaii)](../people/david-hawaii.md), friends, and José, a love.

Flight attendant from 2014 to 2016 (Omni Air): trips of about 21 days, often extended by volunteering on her days off (200 dollars a day) because, separated from Mohamed, she did not want to go home to Raleigh. It is the transition of her divorce. Her close colleagues are [Veronica](../people/veronica.md) and [Kim](../people/kim.md), who has lupus: when Kim has a crisis in flight and is left in a hospital in the Dominican Republic without the airline calling her family, Lillian is shocked by the answer of a quality inspector (« the show must go on »), which ends the magic of flying for her. Kim then leaves the airline for Maui, where she meets David. In 2015,  she discovers Hawaii during a layover and wants to live there. In 2016, tired of partying and travelling, she ends her marriage to Mohamed and moves to Maui, welcomed by David after Kim's last-minute break-up with him.

In January 2020 she leaves Maui to join José, a Spanish teacher, in Gillette, Wyoming (about 5 months, hated by Lillian). He returns to Spain; the COVID closes the borders, and Lillian, obsessed for years with living in Alaska, goes there as an agency nurse.
