# 01 — Origins and early childhood

## Dates

- **Start**: 1976 (her parents meet, before Lillian's birth)
- **End**: summer 1985, when the family does not go back after a stay in France
- **Lillian's age**: from 0 to about 6

## Era summary

This era tells the story of Lillian's origins: a love story between an American GI, Mark, and a young French au pair, Béatrice, who meet in 1976 in a GI bar in Frankfurt. Lillian is born three years later, in 1979, at the military hospital in Heidelberg.

She grows up in Germany in an American military family, with her bedtime rhymes and her first language, English. Her sister Gwen is born in 1982. Mark's debts and financial difficulties, and probably a drinking problem, make family life less and less bearable.

In summer 1985, the family goes on holiday to Saint-Brevin, to Béatrice's parents. Béatrice's father suggests she stay in France as long as Mark's situation does not improve. It does not improve: the holiday becomes the break-up. Lillian takes the separation from her father very hard.
