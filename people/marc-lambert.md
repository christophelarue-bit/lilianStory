## Relationship with Lillian

Lillian never liked him. According to her, he always sulked and spoiled all her birthdays, and was unstable, depressive and unwell. Also according to her, it stank that her brother and sister adored him so much, since with a bar on the beach and many gifts, it was easy. She felt she was losing her mother. As a child, she did not wonder whether her mother was fulfilled with him.

## Anecdotes and stories

- **The first day at boarding school**: he accompanied her to the boarding school on the first day of term, for the parents-students meeting (Papouche was probably unavailable, a medical appointment perhaps). Leather jacket, tight look, and a 50-franc note slipped to her in front of everyone: "Ah, a stepfather, so cool!" (Lillian about 15, to confirm).
- **The birthdays**: he spoiled all her birthdays. He always sulked and sometimes got angry; to her "why?" he answered « Tu sais très bien pourquoi » [You know very well why], « on va mettre les points sur les i » [let's set things straight], « on va parler de la France » [let's talk about France], then he stopped speaking. Lillian thinks he was depressed.
- **The break-up**: he took back the bike he had bought for the children, the curtains and all the gifts. He also wrote to Papouche to be reimbursed for the money he had invested.
- **His ex-wife**: according to Lillian, when his wife asked for a divorce, before he met Béatrice, he punched her in the face and broke her front teeth. He had two sons. This struck Lillian: "not a good sign". (**Sensitive**)
- Lillian, around 11-12, comes back from Switzerland and finds that Xavier and Gwen talk only about Marc, who buys them Malabar candy and gifts.
