## Relationship with Lillian

They met at the Lycée Grand Air, where both were boarders, at about 15. They connected strongly, partly because each had a difficult relationship with their parents. Sandrine knows Lillian's story and knows her « like a sister ». Sandrine kept following Lillian's life closely. Today Lillian does not call her: she does not want to be a negative weight, since Sandrine has found her balance. Sandrine wrote on Lillian's birthday to arrange a call, and Lillian did not call back, which she says is « nothing against her ».
