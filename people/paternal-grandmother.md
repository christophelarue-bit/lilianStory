## Relationship with Lillian

Lillian bears her first name: she was named after her. Throughout Lillian's childhood in France, from the age of 6, she sent the children a huge parcel of presents every Christmas, something Mark never did. She earned almost nothing and began buying the Christmas gifts in January, with the sales. According to Lillian, a tough person, but generous. She was harsh with her son Mark, whom she often belittled, and very affectionate with Lillian (see "Anecdotes and stories").

## Anecdotes and stories

- Widowed at 36, pregnant with Jackie and already mother of 6, she raised her 7 children alone and worked as a waitress in diners.
- Lillian was named after her.
- **The Christmas parcels**: every Christmas she sent the children in France a huge parcel of gifts, from Lillian's 6 years onward (see [02-01](../periods/02-01-childhood-and-teens-in-france.md)). She earned very little ("like Mom today", says Lillian) and bought the gifts from January, with the sales.
- **Two rapes** (**Sensitive**, recorded soberly, as Lillian learned them after her death): [Larry](larry.md), Mark's older brother, was the result of a rape. A second rape took place after her husband's death: a man entered the house while the children were there. According to Lillian, no child resulted from it. The man was described as Black (wording as reported to Lillian).
- **Affection**: very affectionate, "the mega hug", "I love you", more than Lillian's French grandparents, who were far more reserved. She took Lillian in her arms and held her tight. Lillian's wording: « ma grand-mère maternelle », a slip for the paternal one, as the context shows.
- **Her visit to France** (around 1991-1992, Lillian 11-12; Lillian first said 9-10): she came once to Saint-Brevin with « Jane » (probably Joanne, to confirm), stayed in a hotel for about two weeks and spent time with the children. Lillian has only a vague memory of it. It is during this stay that she understood that Béatrice, Mark's ex-wife (to confirm), was seeing someone, [Marc Lambert](marc-lambert.md); back home she told Mark.
- **Her house**: unlike Mamouche, mistress of the house with her armchair, whom everybody waited for before sitting down at table, Grandma cooked for everyone, served them, and then sat on the floor herself, at about 70. Lillian, shocked, looked at her father; nobody else was shocked, and it seemed normal to her (Mark's siblings, five to seven of them, were in the house).
- **The dishes**: when Lillian offered to help with the dishes, Grandma got angry: did she think she was too old and needed assistance? She refused any help.
- **Her treatment of Mark** (according to Lillian): she kept belittling her son, in front of Lillian when she was very small. She told him he had failed at everything, that it was his fault, that he did not have his children since he was no longer with Béatrice, and that he looked like a wreck: « Regarde-toi, Mark, tu ressembles à rien, t'as tout raté » [Look at yourself, Mark, you look like nothing, you failed at everything]. How Mark reacted is to be clarified.
- **The family lie**: when Lillian was little, her grandmother told her that Larry's father was ill. Lillian learned the reality after her grandmother's death.
