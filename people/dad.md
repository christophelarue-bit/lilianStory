## Identity

- **First name**: Mark. Lillian calls him « Papa » [Dad].
- **Role in Lillian's life**: father
- **Nationality / origin**: American, born in Syracuse (New York State)
- **Age**: 46 in 1998, so born around 1952 and about 74 today (date of birth to ask)
- **Occupation**: soldier in the US Army (a GI), notably deployed in Iraq (dates and rank to clarify)
- **Place of residence**: Syracuse, New York State, United States. Still alive.
- **Places linked to his life path**: military base in Frankfurt, Germany (1976); military hospital in Heidelberg (Lillian's birth, 1979)

## Relationships between people

- [Mom](mom.md) (Béatrice): they met in 1976 in a GI bar in Frankfurt, love at first sight on his side. A couple, three children, then a de facto separation from summer 1985 onward, when she stays in France with the children. Mark told Lillian in 1998 that he was still in love with Béatrice, and he still is today.
- [Gwen](gwen.md): daughter, born in 1982.
- [Xavier](xavier.md): son, born after summer 1985 (Béatrice was pregnant at the time of the return to France).
- [Clara](clara.md): his partner in Syracuse, whom he met after Lillian encouraged him to meet women. She asked him to choose between her and Lillian, and he did not answer.
- [Papouche (the maternal grandfather)](maternal-grandfather.md): Béatrice's father, he suggested she stay in France as long as Mark's financial situation did not improve.

## Relationship with Lillian

- Present in her life until 1985, that is, during her first six years.
- After the family leaves for France, the relationship becomes a long-distance one. The details (calls, letters, visits) are to be asked.
- At 18, Lillian goes back to the United States to be closer to him. This is the subject of a separate period: [03-01 — Syracuse: finding Dad](../periods/03-01-syracuse-finding-dad.md).
- Around 1995, at 16, Lillian goes on holiday to New York with Gwen to see him.
- On 11 September 1998 she arrives in the United States to live with him in North Syracuse. He is not at the airport, because he went to the wrong one, but he is more than delighted to welcome her.
- He is 46. For Lillian, time stopped when she was little: he speaks only about the past and about her mother, and tells her in the first days that it is hard to look at her because she looks too much like Béatrice, whom he is still in love with.
- She is saddened by his very simple routine: watching films and reading almost all the time. She remembers seeing him look at the wall, just to pass the time.
- He calls her « Soussoune » (his nickname for her) and bounces her on his knees at 18, as when she was little. He is afraid she will take the bus at 18, as if she were 10. Lillian wants to make up for the lost time and plays along, being still a little girl in her head, but she never told herself that time could be made up.
- He has remorse for having lost his family, but no distance on the fact he could have taken his destiny in hand. He never explained to Lillian why he did not set up a home for his wife and children in the United States, or join them in France if that was better.
- Lillian encourages him to place a personal ad: he meets Clara. He is happy with her at the start. About a year and a half after Clara enters his life, she asks him to choose between her and Lillian. He tells Lillian about the ultimatum, but when she asks what he has decided, he hesitates and does not answer. Lillian leaves Syracuse on that non-answer.

## Impact on Lillian's life

- It is because of him, his debts and his financial problems, that the family cannot hold on in Germany and that Béatrice returns to France in 1985. Lillian therefore grows up in France, far from her father.
- Lillian is the only one of the siblings to have spoken English before French. She still has a slight American accent from it, which people find charming.
- Through him, she has a dual Franco-American identity.
- The return to Syracuse at 18 is a major life choice, born of the need to be closer to him.
