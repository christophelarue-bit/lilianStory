## Anecdotes and stories

- **The car**: he lent Lillian his car. It was dirty, so she cleaned the inside and had the outside washed before handing back the keys to thank him. David took her in his arms, lifted her up and said « Thank you, honey! ». She stayed stoic (« Ok »), the same reaction as with her uncle John.
- He used to warn others: « Be careful with Lillian, she's a little conservative » (Anne, her nickname), because Lillian is not tactile, having been raised by her French grandparents, who were far more reserved.

- **Yahoo Chat (about 1999-2002)**: they chatted for about 2 years on Yahoo Chat, from a library where Lillian had only 30 minutes, and had never met in person. His nickname was « Fly Guy » (confident, stylish, very funny). In about January 2002 she left Herkimer for New York to meet him.
- **His family**: his mother spoke only Chinese; his family did not accept her, judging her too bohemian, a bit lost, without a life plan, « pas très stable ». Lillian says they were right: she was lost in New York. For him it was almost a pride that a white girl was interested in him; he felt part of a minority, like Filipinos and Mexicans, uprooted.
- **Since**: they met again in person in about 2018 (Lillian was already in Maui, to clarify: he came, or she?), well after Mohamed. They stayed in contact on Facebook, then WhatsApp, even during the marriage.
- Lillian calls him her first boyfriend (« mon premier petit ami était chinois »), whereas the files said « a little flirtation » (to clarify).
