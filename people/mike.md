## Relationship with Lillian

Mike wanted to be with Lillian when they met; she was not interested, but they stayed friends. She describes him as solid, « square » [reliable, rigorous] and very cynical, Republican (he voted for Trump), with defects and political views she is « completely against ». But she knows he keeps his word, which is why she trusts him with administrative matters.

## Impact on Lillian's life

In 2020, he receives her French passport at his place and sends it to her in Alaska by FedEx. Lillian says he « saved her life » and that she owes him for the rest of her life, even with his faults. She chose him rather than David, whom she loves but did not trust for this task.

## Importance in her life

Key friend of the Hawaii years, and her reliable contact for administrative matters.

## Importance in her story and the shaping of who she is
