## Anecdotes and stories

**Sensitive: to confirm with Lillian before any use in a book**

- **Alcoholism**: for more than 20 years she drank, notably a bottle of gin mixed in a water bottle. Lillian says she was not dying, she was « full alcoolique » and self-destroying. Everyone pretended not to know, even the two doctors of the family (Dominique and Bruno, relations to clarify). Nobody had the strength to go and see her.
- **Visits**: only Béatrice went daily; Papouche went once a week. She died in 2017.
- **The first memory (Lillian at 13)**: on the way to the United States for the first time, Lillian goes to Paris with Papouche, and they find Kakinou completely drunk on the kitchen floor of her apartment, rue de Courcelles. Papouche, very dignified, says nothing, as if it were normal. Lillian felt it was not normal, but was excited to see her father and not worried; she says it is where she learned to push things under the carpet.
