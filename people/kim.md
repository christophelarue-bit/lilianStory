## Relationship with Lillian

A friend and fellow flight attendant at Omni Air, about 10 years older than Lillian, who has lupus. Lillian saved her in a crisis during a flight and stayed all night at her bedside in a hospital in a poor country, until the airline ordered her back to work. Kim then left the airline for Maui. She knew Hawaii and, with David, showed the island to Lillian during a layover in 2015, which gave Lillian "the call of the island".

## Impact on Lillian's life

She is at the origin of Lillian's wish to live in Hawaii. In 2016 she suggested the flatshare with David, then announced at the last minute that she had left him and that Lillian would lose her friendship if she moved in (according to Lillian).

## Anecdotes and stories

- **The chest pains (Sensitive: health)**: during a flight to Puerto Rico or the Dominican Republic, Kim has chest pains and Lillian insists on going to hospital, which Kim first refuses. On landing they go to the emergency room, where a heart attack is first suspected and the doctor wants to keep her. The country is poor: the power fails, the generator comes on, and there is one nurse for 2 floors, so everything takes a very long time. Lillian spends nearly the whole night with Kim, who holds her hand and says, in tears: « if I die, tell my son ». The flight leader then tells Lillian she must go back to work the next day. Furious, she calls the captain in his room; he tells her to go to bed and sleep because they must leave the next day. She flies furious the next day, with the feeling of having abandoned her friend, but had to resume the flights (the flight leader's remark about dismissal is partly lost).
- **After the hospital**: Kim spent 2 or 3 weeks in a hospital in the Dominican Republic, then was transferred to Texas, where she went through diagnosis after diagnosis (heart, digestive tract, others). The final diagnosis was lupus, an autoimmune disease with no cure, which comes in waves and affects the heart, stomach and muscles: it was the cause of her crisis at altitude. Kim then resigned from Omni Air and went to Maui, to her aunt's flat, where she met [David](david-hawaii.md) and lived with him for about 6 months (less than Lillian first thought).
- **Abandoned abroad**: the airline did not contact her family or her three children, though it had plenty of free tickets. Lillian was shocked. See [05-01](../periods/05-01-flight-attendant.md).

- To confirm, because Kay and Kim are two different women: in Seward, Alaska, she lives in the same building as Lillian, with a gay friend: they often have pyjama parties (see [05-03](../periods/05-03-nurse-in-alaska.md)).
- 2015: during a layover in Hawaii (Lillian then works for Omni Air), Kim and David show her the island. Lillian wants to come back and live there.
