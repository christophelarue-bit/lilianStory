## Anecdotes and stories

- **Time with Mark**: according to Lillian, she knew her father from 0 to 5 (Germany until 1980, then the United States), then for about a year from 18 when she came back to see him, and she stayed in regular contact with him while she was in the States during her marriage to Mohamed.
- **Self-confidence and Xavier**: she lacked self-confidence and put her brother Xavier on a pedestal (he recited La Fontaine's fables by heart and understood mathematics, which she did not), taking him along on her outings with her friends (see [xavier.md](xavier.md)).
- **After Papouche's death (autumn 2018, Sensitive)**: she goes back to the United States, to Hawaii, where she lives in Maui. She sees a psychiatrist two or three times to talk about Papouche's death, but with her work (40 hours a week or more) she cannot find slots; she cancels once because she is needed at work, the secretary tells her they can no longer take her, and she stops going.
- **Announcing Papouche's death to Gwen (September 2018, Sensitive)**: Lillian decides that it must not be Béatrice, who is not in a state to do it, and phones Gwen herself, at home (« Luce »/Luz/Lucé, spelling to clarify). Gwen screams, a scream of atrocious pain that lasts an eternity, as if a part of her had died, as if one of her children had been run over. She cannot speak. About a month later, she takes steps to leave Thomas.
- **Croatia and Papouche's death (2018, Sensitive)**: about ten days in Croatia with Béatrice, 2-3 weeks after the Luce scene, a trip ruined by it (the family had pooled money for it). A few weeks later, the day after Papouche's cancer is said to have come back, they quarrel at the café « 7 Beaufort » in Saint-Brevin: Béatrice does not understand her need for big trips. Half an hour later her aunt [Lou](lou.md) phones: Papouche is dead. Lillian walks, then runs to her mother, in hyperventilation and a panic attack, afraid she will faint. Béatrice answers: « Lillian, on n'a pas le temps pour les émotions. On doit aller voir Mamouche ». At the house, Mamouche is stoic, Lou is agitated, and Béatrice takes charge (see [maternal-grandfather.md](maternal-grandfather.md)).
- **The 2018 family reunion (July 2018, Sensitive)**: the family gathers for a few days at Gwen and Thomas's large house (7 bedrooms, pool; place « Luce »/Lucé? to clarify), for Gwen wanted the whole family together. Present: Béatrice, Lillian, Xavier, Mark (one of his first trips to France, to meet his new grandson [Clément](clement.md), born in June), Gwen, Thomas and the children. During a lunch on the terrace, Gwen and Thomas go inside and have a loud fight; Thomas shouts and insults the family (« une famille de dégénérés » [a family of degenerates]). Gwen, Béatrice and Lillian cry. Thomas leaves for two days of hiking in the mountains with [Alexandre](alexandre.md); Lillian goes for a walk in a park with Mark and Xavier. Mark returns to the United States the next day, at the planned end of his stay. Lillian and Béatrice leave earlier for about ten days in Croatia, ruined by the scene. Béatrice cries all along, while Thomas sends her messages saying that she should manage Gwen herself and that she will see she cannot manage her money. At the same time, at the table, Thomas hits Alexandre with a towel (about his behaviour at table; to clarify).
- Marc Lambert takes her to boarding school one first day of term and slips her a 50-franc note in front of everyone, but spoils all her birthdays and, after the break-up with Béatrice, takes back the gifts (see [marc-lambert.md](marc-lambert.md)).
- Around 1991-1992 (11-12), her American grandmother visits Saint-Brevin for two weeks, in a hotel (vague memory). Béatrice's boyfriend then is [Marc Lambert](marc-lambert.md), who has a bar on the beach: Gwen and Xavier adore him, Lillian never liked him and feels she is losing her mother.
- At her American grandmother's, she is shocked to see her, at about 70, cook for everyone then sit on the floor; when she offers to wash the dishes, Grandma gets angry. Nobody else is shocked (see [paternal-grandmother.md](paternal-grandmother.md)). The French side is the opposite: Mamouche has her armchair and everyone waits for her to sit.
- **Two ways of showing affection**: raised by her French grandparents, reserved (no hugs, no « je t'aime » said aloud, though felt), she is shocked by her American family's hugs: her uncle John squeezes her the first time they meet, her grandmother gives the « méga hug », David lifts her in his arms. She stays stoic. When she hugs Papouche goodbye, he is as frozen as she was; her mother tells her it is not their culture. She calls it becoming American.
- As a very small child, she hears her paternal grandmother belittle her father, telling him he has failed at everything and that it is his fault if he no longer has his children (see [paternal-grandmother.md](paternal-grandmother.md)).
- From 6, every Christmas, her American grandmother (Grandma Lillian) sends a huge parcel of gifts to France, which her father never did. Grandma earned very little and bought the gifts in January, with the sales (see [paternal-grandmother.md](paternal-grandmother.md)).
- At 19, she knits a Doctor Who scarf for her father, a fan of the series. Mamouche had taught her to knit between 7 and 10, and Mark wore the scarf (see [03-01](../periods/03-01-syracuse-finding-dad.md)).
- The only one of the siblings to have spoken English before French, she has kept a slight American accent, which people find charming.
- Around 1995, at 16, she goes on holiday to New York with Gwen to see their father.
- About 1999, after the Price Chopper cashier job in Syracuse, she looks for schools in the Yellow Pages and enters Job Corps, a free programme created under Clinton for very disadvantaged youth. Being one of the only white students, she gets a « social class shock » and a wake-up call: she realises she should be grateful for her upbringing, and stops playing the "no dad" card. Her friends there: Ivan from Ecuador and his brother David; the supervisor was Miss V ([period 03-02](../periods/03-02-job-corps.md)).
- On 11 September 1998, at 18, she goes back to the United States to be closer to her father ([period 03-01](../periods/03-01-syracuse-finding-dad.md)). He is not at the airport because he went to the wrong one. She finds him full of remorse, stuck in the past and in love with her mother, with a simple routine of films and books. For her, time stopped when she was little. She wants to make up for the lost time and plays along, being still a little girl in her head, but never told herself that time could be made up. She encourages him to place a personal ad, and he meets Clara.
- She has her French baccalauréat but tries high school in the United States at her father's suggestion; she lasts 2 weeks (shock after her boarding school in La Baule and the French system). She then works as a cashier at a supermarket 20 minutes' walk away, where she has a crush on a coworker, one of her first crushes. She resigns after a misunderstanding and has avoided that store ever since.

- **Her beliefs (the source)**: Lillian believes in a « source » (Mother Nature, the universe, God) that is beyond human understanding, of which everyone is a part and with which everyone is interconnected: « the sum of atoms », a force, an energy. She does not believe in nothingness after death: the body and the energies return to the source. She does not think she will find Papouche and Mamouche as they were, but that their energies join hers. She says this belief keeps her alive (the start of the sentence was lost in the recording). She was a practising Christian child, but stopped church at 12 (see [02-01](../periods/02-01-childhood-and-teens-in-france.md)). Nature has always been her therapy.

- **The eldest child who took care of the others**: see the babysitting and the birthday money in [02-01](../periods/02-01-childhood-and-teens-in-france.md). The psychologist's analogy: Lillian was for Gwen and Xavier what Julia is today for Gwen.
- **Nursing career**: she studies nursing in the United States from 2006 to 2008, works as a nurse in hospices and nursing homes (Seasons Hospice, Baltimore) from about 2009 to 2013, then six months in an office for a for-profit hospice agency (verifying that dying patients qualify, with quotas of 25 admissions a day), where she feels like « the agent of death ». Her American grandmother's death in 2013 and the stomach aches make her change course. See [04-02](../periods/04-02-nurse-in-hospices-baltimore.md).
- **Flight attendant years (2014-2016)**: separated from Mohamed, she avoided going home to Raleigh, volunteered to work on her days off (200 dollars a day) and could be away for 3 months; she calls it the transition of her divorce. The airline's abandonment of her colleague [Kim](kim.md) in the Dominican Republic and the answer « the show must go on » broke the magic of flying for her. Close friends: [Veronica](veronica.md) and Kim. See [05-01](../periods/05-01-flight-attendant.md).
- **Therapy and the family (draft, Sensitive: to confirm with Lillian before any use in a book)**: recently, after two sessions with a psychologist, he stopped her after almost an hour spent talking about her brother, her mother and her sister, and not about her. In his view, the relationships are toxic and they are « drowning » her: he advised her to send a message to the three and to stop answering the phone every day. He noted that Xavier (about 40) and Gwen (about 44) will not change, and that only she is sinking. When she mentioned Xavier's threat to commit suicide, he called it manipulation; he is outraged by Gwen's care of her children (« negligence »; Béatrice sends Lillian papers to defend Gwen). He told her she is the mother of the family, whereas Christophe observes that for Béatrice she is a friend and for Xavier a mother since the beginning. Her main problem, he says, is guilt: she feels obliged to answer, afraid that they will blame her or that something will happen to Xavier or Gwen, and carries everything alone. Her birthday was spoiled and she no longer cares about Christmas. Her former remedy was to work in the United States (nursing, double salary).
